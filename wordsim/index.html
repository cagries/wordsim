How to play?

Find the hidden word by guessing one common English word at a time.

  • First guess: You start with no prior information, guess anything!
  • Similarity: Higher scores mean the words are closer in meaning, with 100 being the maximum.
  • Ranking: Lower is better. #1 is the hidden word, while “cold” means the guess is outside the closest 1000 words.
  • Hints: Word hints reveal increasingly close words. The category hint unlocks after 5 accepted guesses.
